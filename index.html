Who am I

I am an artist and a technologist at heart. Since childhood, I've been drawn to creating — sketching, designing, and exploring anything that let me express ideas visually. As technology evolved, so did my curiosity. In my teen years, I discovered the world of web customization and spent hours playing with HTML to personalize my Myspace page. That moment opened my eyes: I could create with technology just as I did with art, and share it with the world. That realization inspired me to pursue a bachelor's degree in Computer Information Systems, blending creativity with technical problem‑solving. Today, I continue to explore the space where art, design, and technology meet.
